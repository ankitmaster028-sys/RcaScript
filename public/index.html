How to Use

Follow these simple steps to get started — designed for beginners

1

Choose Your Mode

Click on Single Mode for 1 account or Bulk Mode for up to 5 accounts at once.

2

Enter Login Details

Type your User ID (comma-separated for Bulk) and Password. Then click Login.

3

Add Coins (If Needed)

Click Add Coins, enter the Pass Key, and add up to 500 coins per request.

4

Complete Tasks

Click any Solve button next to a level, skill, or activity. It completes instantly with zero delay.

5

Track Progress

Watch the live progress bar and logs. Refresh the dashboard to see updated completion status.
