How to Use

Follow these 4 simple steps to complete your IELTS modules in seconds

1. Pick Your Mode

Click Single Mode if you want to manage just your own account. Click Bulk Mode if you need to run tasks on up to 5 accounts together. Bulk mode shows all users side-by-side in one dashboard.

2. Login Your Account

Enter your User ID and Password, then hit Login. In Bulk Mode, type up to 5 IDs separated by commas (e.g. 12345, 67890, 11111). The system will authenticate all accounts and load their levels.

3. Complete Tasks Instantly

Your dashboard shows all 6 levels (A1 to C2). Click Solve Level to finish an entire level, Solve Skill for a specific skill, or Solve Q for individual activities. Everything completes with zero delay — no waiting.

4. Add Coins (Optional)

Need more coins? Click Add Coins in the sidebar, enter your Pass Key, and type any amount from 1 to 500. Coins are added instantly to your account. No coins are deducted for completing tasks — it's completely free.
